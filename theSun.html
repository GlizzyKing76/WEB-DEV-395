What is the Sun?

Our Sun is a yellow dwarf star that's 4.5 billion years old. It is the center of our solar system Our Sun has a radius of about 435000 miles (700000 kilometers) which, compared to many stars, is relatively average. It is about 93 million miles (150 million kilometers) from Earth however without its energy, life would no exist in our home planet. The Sun's core can go up to 27 million degrees fahrenheit (15 million degrees celcius).

Solar Flares

When the Sun's activity cycle goes to it's peak know as solar maximum, we start to see Sunspots. Sunspots are eruptions called solar flares, solar activity can release huge amounts of energy and particles that affects us in Earth. They can interfere with satellites, GPS, and radio communications.
