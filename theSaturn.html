Welcome to Saturn

What is Saturn?

Saturn is a gas giant much like Jupiter, it is a massive ball made of mostly hydrogen and helium. Saturn is 9 times wider than Earth with an equatorial diameter of 74897 miles (120500 kilometers) and it has an average distance of 886 million miles (1.4 billion kilometers) from the sun. Saturn has a rotation duration of 10.7 hours but it takes about 29.4 Earth years for it to complete its orbit around the Sun. It has an axis tilt of 26.73 degrees which is similar to Earth's 23.4 degree tilt.

Saturn's Rings

Saturn's rings are made of billions of small chunks of ice and rock, its particles mostly range from tiny, dust-sized icy grains, to house-sized chunks. They extent up to 175000 miles (282000 kilometers) from the planet however it's vertical height is as small as 30 feet (10 meters) in the main rings
