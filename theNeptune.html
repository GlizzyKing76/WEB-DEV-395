What is Neptune?

Neptune is an ice giant, it is the only planet in our solar system that is not visiable to the naked eye. Neptune is four times wider that Earth and it has an equatorial diameter of 30775 miles (49528 kilometers), it also has average distance of 2.8 billion miles (4.5 billion kilometers) from the Sun. Neptune has a rotation duration of about 16 hours, however it takes about 165 Earth years for it to complete its orbit around the Sun

Neptune's Rings

Neptune has at least 5 main rings and 4 prominent ring arcs that we know of so far. The ring's names are: Galle, Leverrier, Arago, and Adams.
