What is Mercury?

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun, and it has a radius of 1,516 miles (2,439 kilometers). It is about 38% the size of Earth, making it the smallest planet in our solar system. Mercury is a rocky planet, and it has a solid surface made of rock and metal. The planet has a very thin atmosphere, which means that it cannot retain heat. As a result, Mercury experiences extreme temperature fluctuations, with daytime temperatures reaching up to 800 degrees Fahrenheit (427 degrees Celsius) and nighttime temperatures dropping to -290 degrees Fahrenheit (-179 degrees Celsius).

Mercury's Orbit

Mercury has a very short orbital period, taking only 88 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun. This means that a year on Mercury is less than three months long! The planet also has a very slow rotation, taking about 59 Earth days to complete one rotation on its axis. This means that a day on Mercury is longer than its year!
