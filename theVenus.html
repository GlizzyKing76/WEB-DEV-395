What is Venus?

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and the closest planet to Earth. It has a similar structure and size to Earth and is sometimes called Earth's evil twin. It has a thick atmosphere that traps heat, making it the hottest planet in our solar system. The surface of Venus is covered with volcanoes and vast plains and has a surface temperature of about 872 degrees Fahrenheit (467 degrees Celsius) which is hot enough to melt lead.
