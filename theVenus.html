What is Venus?

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and the closest planet to Earth. It has a similar structure and size to Earth and is sometimes called Earth's evil twin. Venus is nearly as big as Earth, its diameter is about 7521 miles (12104 kilometers) while Earth has 7926 miles (12756 kilometers). It has an average distance of 67 million miles (108 million kilometers) from the Sun

Venus' Surface

Venus has a thick atmosphere that traps heat, making it the hottest planet in our solar system. The surface of Venus is covered with volcanoes and vast plains and has a surface temperature of about 872 degrees Fahrenheit (467 degrees Celsius) which is hot enough to melt lead. It has an atmospheric pressure with the same force as being more than 3000 feet underwater on Earth (930 meters).
