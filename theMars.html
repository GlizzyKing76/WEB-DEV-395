Welcome to Mars

What is Mars?

Mars is about half the size of Earth, it has a radius of 2106 miles (3390 kilometers), and it has an average distance of 142 million miles (228 million kilometers) from the sun. Mars has a similar rotation duration as Earth, Earth being 23.9 hours and Mars being 24.6 hours. Mars has tilt of 25 degrees which is another close similarity it has with Earth, Earth has a 23.4 degree tilt. Mars has a temperatur that can go as high as 70 degrees Fahrenheit (20 degrees Celsius) to as low as -225 degrees fahrenheit (-153 degrees celsius)

Mars' Moons

Mars has two moons, Phobos and Deimos that may actually be captured asteroids. Phobos is the larger of the two and is heavily cratered, it is slowling moving towards mars and will likely crash into the planet or break apart in the next 50 years. Deimos is the smaller of the two and is covered in loose dirt, it is also about half as big as Phobos
